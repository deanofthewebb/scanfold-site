ScanFold support

ScanFold turns a LiDAR iPhone into a 3D scanner that keeps everything on the phone. Walk a room. Watch the surfaces fill in live. Finish, and you have a 3D mesh you can spin, stand inside at 1:1, measure against a scale bar, and export as USDZ, GLB, PLY or OBJ. Every scan is pinned where you made it on a private map, with no account, no server and no upload step. Live 3D reconstruction needs a LiDAR iPhone (12 Pro or later Pro and Pro Max models) and iOS 18 or later.
